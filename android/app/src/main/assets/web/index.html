How breeding works

Choose two different eligible monsters from your stable. Each parent can be used in one breeding per season; after breeding, those two monsters rest until the next season. You can still breed a different pairing during the same season, provided neither selected parent has already bred.

Each breeding costs 1,000 gold, and your stable must have room for the new arrival. Rare genes are difficult to combine, so building an exceptional bloodline may take several generations.

Climb the divisions, build your reputation and fight for the ultimate titles. Win bouts to earn points, rise through the ranks and qualify for the European and World Championships. Competition Rules

Season formatEach division has 20 competitors and 19 bouts per monster.

Promotion & relegationThe top three normally earn promotion where a higher division exists; the bottom three are relegated where a lower division exists.

Premier entryA monster needs at least one special-gene carrier allele or an expressed special gene to enter Premier.

ChampionshipsWin Premier to qualify for Europe. Win Europe to qualify for the World Championship; Primal Apex is not required.

🧬 Gene Codex

Learn what each special gene does, recognise its colour and symbol, and discover the combinations worth pursuing when breeding your next champion.

Solid coloured icon = gene expressed · Dashed coloured outline = gene carrier (one copy) · Grey icon = absent

What to look for: A carrier has one copy of a gene. A gene is expressed when the monster inherits two copies. Expressed genes grant their own combat bonuses; combinations add further synergy: two expressed genes give +1 to every combat stat, three give +2, and all four give +3 and unlock Primal Apex.
